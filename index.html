Get started FAQ

What is this for?

Whether you need a shareable invoice, a printable report, a resume, or a saved copy of a webpage, dompdfui gives you a straightforward way to produce PDFs with attractive default styling. It focuses on helping you complete the task — not on settings you don't need.

How it helps people

  • Turn online content into offline copies you can keep or print.
  • Create professional-looking documents without design experience.
  • Save time: one-click export and consistent formatting across pages.

Common questions

Do I need any software?
No — dompdfui is a web-oriented interface. If you're using an installation that includes the interface, everything you need is in the app. If you are using an online demo, you only need a browser.
Can I change how the PDF looks?
Yes — there are simple layout options like page size, margins, and basic typographic scale. The defaults are chosen to look good for most documents.
Is my content private?
If you're using a hosted demo, check that service's privacy policy. For local or self-hosted instances, your documents stay on your machine or server.
